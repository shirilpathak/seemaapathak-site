Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a consultation take?

Standard consultations are 45-60 minutes. Urgent consultations are 30 minutes. Spiritual healing sessions may take 60-90 minutes.

What information do I need to provide?

You'll need your exact date, time, and place of birth for accurate astrology readings. For other consultations, just bring your specific concerns.

Are consultations confidential?

Absolutely! All consultations are 100% confidential. Your personal information and discussion details are never shared with anyone.

What if I need to reschedule?

You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your appointment. Emergency rescheduling is available for urgent situations.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes! I offer consultations via Zoom, WhatsApp video, and phone calls for clients worldwide.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI, Paytm, Google Pay, and other digital payment methods. Payment details are shared after booking confirmation.
